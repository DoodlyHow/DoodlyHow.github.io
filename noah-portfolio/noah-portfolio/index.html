About Me

Hi, I'm Noah.

I am a senior at California State University, East Bay, pursuing a degree in Game Design with a strong passion for creating immersive player experiences. I am seeking opportunities as a Level Designer or in Storyboarding, where I can apply both my creative vision and technical skills.

My background includes hands-on experience with Unreal Engine, JavaScript, Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, and Figma. I also have working knowledge of Maya, Blender, and Arduino, allowing me to approach projects from both artistic and technical perspectives. Additionally, I have completed video editing coursework and earned two certificates of completion from the iD Tech Pathway in Level Design and Cinematics.

I am continuously expanding my skill set and am eager to contribute, learn, and grow within the game design industry.

Combat Arena · Blockout · Unreal Engine

Destroyed Command Center

A combat arena blocked out inside a wrecked command center. The roof has caved in, and the collapsed beams, slabs and scattered rubble become cover across the main floor.

Stairs and raised walkways give players high ground over the open center, while low cover and broken ramps keep the floor fights moving. The walkthrough shows the layout at player scale with a third-person character.

3D Environment · Interior

Steampunk Attic Study

A cozy attic room under a curved roof, split into a work area and a lounge. Gears, copper pipes, a grandfather clock and a crank-legged worktable carry the steampunk theme through the furniture.

The clay renders show the layout and props without textures. The lighting pass tests sunlight from the tall windows, which throws window-pane shadows across the floor and pulls the eye to the central table.

Zones
Study desk, worktable, lounge, fireplace
Focus
Layout, props, light direction
Scale
